# NOMGR Documentation

Curtains, please.

As we all approach America’s 250th birthday, I thought I’d offer my nation and my people a birthday gift, wrapped in a big red bow.

The fundamental basis of this particular barter system is the common denominator. The one single criterion for this is scarcity, and that’s why I chose gold. America’s got swagger, and this I know. But the problem here is that American swag is not scarce. American swag is infinite, so it does not qualify. You could alternatively choose silver or platinum. Really, any scarce resource will do. In America we have plenty of physical gold as well as the ability to acquire plenty more. The Canadian province of Alberta has crude, and other provinces have soft timber. Market gold spot price is easy to look up. So is soft timber and crude oil. I chose the microgram gold price because the gold milligram price is something like $0.13 or $0.14, and currently the American nickel and dime are too valuable to retire from the currency system. Here I made gold legal tender for a simple reason. It guarantees a fair market price for all goods and services as measured by gold. If you want to try something else, go ahead. It’s pretty simple here. If you have some elaborate way of finding the fair market value of your goods and services, go ahead and try it out.
